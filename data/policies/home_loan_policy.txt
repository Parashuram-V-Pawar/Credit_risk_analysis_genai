HOME LOAN POLICY

Eligibility Rules:

1. Minimum Age: 21 years
2. Maximum Age at Loan Maturity: 70 years
3. Minimum CIBIL Score: 650
4. Debt to Income Ratio should be below 50%
5. Stable employment history preferred
6. Minimum employment length: 1 year

Approval Guidelines:

* CIBIL above 750:
  Preferred customer category

* CIBIL 700 to 750:
  Standard approval category

* CIBIL 650 to 699:
  Conditional approval category

* CIBIL below 650:
  Manual review required

Risk Indicators:

* More than 2 loan defaults
* Debt to income ratio above 60%
* Frequent loan enquiries
* Low disposable income

Collateral Benefits:

* Property ownership improves approval probability
* High asset value reduces risk classification
