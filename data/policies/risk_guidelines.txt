RISK CLASSIFICATION GUIDELINES

LOW RISK

Conditions:

* CIBIL above 750
* No defaults
* Debt to income ratio below 30%
* Stable employment

Recommended Action:
Approve

---

MEDIUM RISK

Conditions:

* CIBIL between 650 and 750
* One previous default
* Debt to income ratio between 30% and 50%

Recommended Action:
Conditional Approval

---

HIGH RISK

Conditions:

* CIBIL below 650
* Multiple defaults
* Debt to income ratio above 50%
* Unstable employment

Recommended Action:
Reject or Manual Review
