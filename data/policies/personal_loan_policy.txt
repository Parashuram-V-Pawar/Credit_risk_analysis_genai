PERSONAL LOAN POLICY

Eligibility Rules:

1. Minimum monthly income: 25000
2. Minimum CIBIL Score: 680
3. Employment stability required
4. Existing EMI burden below 45%

Approval Guidelines:

* Income above 100000:
  Premium segment

* Income between 50000 and 100000:
  Standard segment

* Income below 50000:
  High review segment

Rejection Conditions:

* CIBIL below 600
* More than 3 previous defaults
* Debt to income ratio above 70%
* Disposable income below 10000

Risk Notes:

Personal loans carry higher risk than secured loans and require stricter underwriting.
